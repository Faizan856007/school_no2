Second Saturdays and make-up days

Second Saturday is a holiday. A Saturday is worked only for CBSE, sports or a lost-day make-up — notice at least seven days before. Buses run that day. For personal leave use the parent app; a gazette restricted holiday is not an automatic school holiday.
