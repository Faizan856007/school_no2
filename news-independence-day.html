Independence Day Celebration — 15 August 2026

Circular No. GVIS/ACT/2026/15  |  Date: 15 August 2026  |  Issued by: Cultural Committee

To: Students, staff and parents

The 79th Independence Day was observed on the main lawn with flag hoisting, the national anthem, house march-past and student performances.

1. Programme (as conducted)

TimeItemIn-charge
7:45 AMAssembly on the main lawnHead Girl / Head Boy
8:00 AMFlag hoisting & National AnthemPrincipal
8:15 AMMarch-past (all four houses)Sports Department
8:30 AMChoir, poems and a short playMusic & Drama
9:20 AMRefreshmentsCafeteria

2. Attendance & uniform

Attendance was compulsory for Classes I–XII. Students reported in formal school uniform with house badges. Primary dispersal was at 10:00 AM; Senior School followed the regular Saturday transport.

3. Photographs

Selected photographs are displayed in the foyer till 22 August and in the school gallery online. Parents who wish to collect printed copies may write to the class teacher by Friday.

4. Note of thanks

The Principal thanks the Cultural Committee, NCC / Scouts volunteers, and parents who joined the morning assembly.

Sd/–
Cultural Committee
Green Valley International School, New Delhi
