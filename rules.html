School rules

Code of conduct for students and parents. If a line here is shorter than the diary, the diary wins.

DayAttendanceConductParentsDiscipline

Every child carries the school diary. Three unsigned remarks in a fortnight bring a Coordinator note — not a public scolding.

School day

ItemTime / rule
Gate 1 (students)Opens 7:25 AM · late after 8:00 AM marked in the app
Assembly7:50 AM, except a listed exam morning
Dispersal2:10 PM · Nursery/KG 12:40 PM on written request
Second SaturdayClosed, unless a circular 7 days ahead
BirthdayOne toffee each; no cake in class; no gifts to staff

Attendance

  • 75% is the CBSE floor. Below that, a school or board exam may be withheld.
  • Leave on the parent app before 8:30 AM.
  • Three days or more: medical certificate on return.
  • A child sent home ill needs a fitness note the next day.

Uniform & devices

  • Full uniform as on the Uniform page. Name tapes on every piece.
  • Phones stay home till Class XI. XI–XII deposit by 8 AM.
  • No camera-watch. No recording a classmate or teacher.
  • Confiscated phone returns only to a parent.

Behaviour

  • No bullying, ragging, caste or religious slur.
  • No circulating another child’s photo.
  • Labs: coat and goggles when told. Library: no phone.
  • Language in the corridor is language you would use at home in front of a grandparent.

Parents

  • Meet teachers in the published slot, not at the gate.
  • Do not discuss another child.
  • Complaint in writing: class teacher → Coordinator → Principal.
  • POCSO / safety can skip the ladder: [email protected]

Discipline ladder

1Diary note

Class teacher. Parent signs the next day.

2Written warning

Coordinator. Copy on file.

3Parent meeting

Within three working days.

4Suspension / TC

Violence, continued bullying, or a forged paper — in writing.
