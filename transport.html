School transport

Optional GPS buses. Routes lock in March. A WhatsApp “stop” is not a stop until the office prints it.

Every bus: trained driver, lady attendant till Class V, first-aid, CCTV, GPS. Speed cap. The office reads the log. Parent sees the same mobile on the app.

Boarding rules

  • Five minutes early. The bus does not reverse into a new lane on a call.
  • One named adult at drop (app). We do not hand a child to an unknown driver.
  • Class IX–XII may walk from the last stop with a signed form. Primary may not.
  • Stop change: written, three working days.
  • Seat belts where fitted. No standing on the last step. No messy food.
  • Monsoon / pollution: if the Delhi circular says so, we run late or close — on the app, not on rumour.

If a bus is late or breaks down

Attendant calls the first five numbers on that route. A spare vehicle is sent from campus. Do not ask the child to take a cab alone. In-charge: Mr. Harish Yadav · [email protected] · +91 98765 43210 (ask for bus).
