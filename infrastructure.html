Laboratories

Physics, Chemistry, Biology (senior), one composite lab for VI–VIII, two computer labs. Practicals for XI–XII are held here — we do not send a board batch to another campus. Lab coat and goggles when the teacher says so. Breakage is billed to accounts before the TC.

Visit and dispersal

  • Visitor pass at Gate 2. Parents do not walk into a classroom unannounced.
  • Dispersal: bus, authorised adult, or own cycle from Class IX with a signed form.
  • Parking on campus. Do not stop on the service road at 2:10 PM.
