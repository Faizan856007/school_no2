PRIMARY SCHOOL

Nursery to Class V

The early years are language, number sense, play, and a room where a child may ask the same question twice. English is the medium; Hindi begins in Nursery.

Two teachers sit with Nursery and KG in the first term. Homework, if any, is 20 minutes — never work that needs an hour of a parent.

  • Phonics and storytelling every morning
  • Mathematics with objects, then paper
  • EVS in the garden and kitchen
  • Art, music, library and games after lunch
  • No ranking or public comparison of marks
Apply for 2026–27

Promotion & parent contact

Promotion to the next class is based on the year’s observation notes and, from Class III, two written reviews. A child who needs more time is discussed with parents in February — not as a surprise in March. Class teachers write a short note every Friday on the parent app.
