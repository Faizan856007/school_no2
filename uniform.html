Campus shop, Gate 2 · 9 AM – 1 PM (1–20 April and every Saturday). Two city shops named on the offer letter. A pavement “full set” is turned away if the shade is wrong. ID card is part of the uniform; duplicate ₹ 150.

Rules that save money

  • Second-hand NCERT allowed if unmarked and the year/code is on this year’s list. Workbooks and diary must be new.
  • No forced brand of notebook. Brown cover; name and class on page 1.
  • Lost diary ₹ 150 at the shop. The diary is the leave form till ERP is live.
  • Bag weight (Primary) — we weigh twice a term. Extra guides stay at home.
  • If a shop says “school recommended only our set”, write to the Principal. We name two shops on the offer letter; that is not a monopoly.
