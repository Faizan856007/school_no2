Admissions Open for Academic Session 2026–27

Circular No. GVIS/ADM/2026-27/08  |  Date: 12 August 2026  |  Issued by: Office of the Principal

To: Parents & prospective students

Registration for Nursery to Class XII is now open. Seats are limited. Kindly complete the online form and visit the Admissions Desk with documents listed below.

1. Who may apply

Applications are invited for Nursery, KG, Classes I to X, and Class XI (Science, Commerce and Humanities). Age for Nursery as on 31 March 2027 should be 3 years and above. For Class XI, provisional admission is offered on the basis of Class X pre-board / board marks.

2. How to apply

  1. Step 1 — Online form: Submit the enquiry form on the school website or at the front desk.
  2. Step 2 — Documents: Bring originals and one photocopy set (list below).
  3. Step 3 — Interaction: A 20-minute meeting is scheduled by SMS / phone.
  4. Step 4 — Offer: Selected families receive a written offer with fee and joining date.
  5. Step 5 — Confirmation: Seat is held only after the registration fee is paid within 7 working days.

3. Documents required

  • Birth certificate (Municipality / Registrar)
  • Aadhaar of child and one parent
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous class report card
  • Transfer Certificate (Class II and above)
  • Residence proof if school transport is required

4. Desk timings & campus visit

The Admissions Desk is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM (closed on second Saturday and gazetted holidays). Parking is at Gate 2. Please reach 10 minutes before the allotted slot. A short campus walk can be combined with the same visit.

5. Fee & concessions

The detailed fee circular is issued with the offer letter. Sibling concession and defence / paramilitary quota may be requested on the form with supporting proof. The school does not collect any donation or capitation fee.
