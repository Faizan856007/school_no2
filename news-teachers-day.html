Teachers' Day Celebration — 5 September 2026

Circular No. GVIS/ACT/2026/18  |  Date: 5 September 2026  |  Issued by: Student Council & Cultural Committee

To: Students, staff and parents

Teachers' Day will be observed on Friday, 5 September 2026, with a special assembly in the auditorium. The regular timetable will be shortened.

1. Programme

TimeItem
8:15 AMParents and guests to be seated
8:20 AMSpecial assembly begins
9:15 AMAssembly ends; short break
9:40 AMShortened teaching periods resume
11:00 AMStaff tea (staff room)

2. Instructions for students

Formal school uniform is compulsory. House captains will collect handwritten notes till 4 September (no expensive gifts, please). Artwork for the foyer must reach the Art Room by Thursday, 4 September, 1:00 PM.

3. For parents

Parents are welcome for the first 45 minutes. Kindly use Gate 1 and be seated by 8:15 AM. Regular teaching continues after the assembly. There is no early dispersal except for Nursery and KG, as notified by the class teacher.

Sd/–
Student Council & Cultural Committee
Green Valley International School, New Delhi
