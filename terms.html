Fee

  • Amounts: Fee structure and the offer letter.
  • Late after the 15th of the instalment month. Cash above ₹ 5,000 is not taken.
  • NEFT must quote the admission number. A bounce / wrong UTR is the parent’s to correct; the desk does not “adjust later” on a call.
  • Sibling: 10% on tuition for the second child only. Defence concession only with ID on the admission form.
  • XI–XII Science lab fee with July. CBSE exam fee extra when the Board bills it.

Bus, ID and private vans

School GPS buses are optional and covered by the indemnity signed at joining. The school is not responsible for a private van you hire. Duplicate ID ₹ 150. A lost bus pass is reported the same day to transport@greenvalley.edu.in.

Conduct, photos, this website

The school may issue a TC for repeated bullying, violence or a forged document — in writing. Photos on this site are with parent consent for the year. Portal logins close with the TC. ERP ledgers and signed letters are the record, not a screenshot. Force majeure (calamity, government order): days may be made up as notified. Privacy Policy.
