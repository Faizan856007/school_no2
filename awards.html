School honours (every year, Annual Day)

  • Scholar badge — 90%+ in the annual (VI–X) or 85%+ in XI, attendance 85%+. No badge for a bought olympiad gold.
  • Subject proficiency — highest in the class in a board / annual subject, minimum 80%.
  • All-rounder cup — one per wing (Primary / Middle / Senior): class + sport/club + conduct. Not for sale.
  • House trophy — August sport + January arts/quiz + attendance of the house.
  • 100% attendance — certificate. Not a fee waiver.
  • Kindness / sewa letter — teacher nominated; no stage ranking of “best child”.
  • Staff — one teaching + one non-teaching mention; not voted on Instagram.

How a name gets on this page

Parent consent, or a CBSE / zonal certificate shown to the office. Bring a photocopy to Gate 2 within 10 days of the event. We take down a name if a parent writes. Fake “national rank” posters are not put on the notice board.
