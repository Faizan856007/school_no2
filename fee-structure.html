How to pay

Parent app, NEFT, or Accounts desk (Gate 2, Mon–Sat 9 AM – 1 PM). Cash above ₹ 5,000 is not taken. Quote the admission number on every transfer.

Concessions

Sibling (second child) 10% on tuition only. Defence / paramilitary — tick the admission form with ID. No cash discount at the gate.

Withdrawal

Tuition till the end of the TC month. Registration and admission fee stay with the school. TC request · Terms.
