Transport (optional)

GPS buses, 0–5 km ₹ 22,000 · 5–10 km ₹ 28,000 · 10–15 km ₹ 34,000 per year. Routes are confirmed in March.

How to pay

Parent app, NEFT, or the accounts desk (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 1 PM). Cash above ₹ 5,000 is not accepted. Late fee after the 15th of the instalment month.

Concessions

Sibling (second child) 10% on tuition. Defence / paramilitary with valid ID — apply on the admission form. No cash discount is offered at the gate.
