How to Pay

Fees can be paid online via the parent portal (net banking, UPI, debit/credit card) or by demand draft at the school accounts office. Instalments are due within the first 10 days of each quarter — April, July, October and January.

Discounts & Scholarships

A 10% sibling discount applies from the second child onward. Merit-based scholarships are available for students scoring above 90% in the previous academic year — apply through the admissions office.
