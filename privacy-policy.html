Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

How Green Valley International School collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 8 August 2026. This is a starter template — please have it reviewed by a legal professional before publishing, and update it to reflect your school's actual data practices.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use this website or apply for admission, we may collect information such as the student's and parent's name, contact number, email address, date of birth, and academic records submitted through our online admission form.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to process admissions, communicate updates about school activities, and maintain student records. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. Access to student records is restricted to authorised school staff only.

4. Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve browsing experience and understand site usage. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites such as social media platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party websites.

6. Your Rights

Parents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's personal data held by the school by writing to the administrative office.

Terms of Use

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this website, you agree to be bound by these terms of use. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Content Accuracy

While we strive to keep information on this website accurate and up to date, the school does not guarantee the completeness or accuracy of all content, including fee structures and academic calendars, which are subject to change.

3. Use of Content

All content on this website, including text, images and logos, is the property of Green Valley International School and may not be reproduced without prior written permission.

4. Admission Decisions

Submission of an online admission form does not guarantee enrolment. Admission is subject to seat availability, eligibility criteria and the school's assessment process.

5. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at info@greenvalley.edu.in or +91 98765 43210.
