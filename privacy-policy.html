Privacy policy

Students, parents and staff. Website, parent portal and paper forms at Gate 2. We do not sell data to advertisers.

Collect Share Photos Rights

School rules + CBSE returns + the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as it applies to a school. Aadhaar is seen at admission, never printed on a notice board.

1

What we collect

Name, date of birth, gender, address, mobile, email, academic records, attendance, fee receipts, transport route if you opt in, medical notes you give us, and photographs for the ID card, yearbook and (with consent) the website.

2

Why

Admission, timetable, attendance, fee, report cards, PTM, transport, infirmary, and statutory returns to CBSE / local authorities. Optional: house, club, alumni register.

3

Who can see it

Class teacher, Coordinator and office on a need-to-know basis. The parent app shows only your ward. Staff must not copy student lists onto a personal phone. Bus GPS: transport desk + that child’s parent.

4

Photographs and CCTV

Assembly and event photos may appear here and on official social pages. Write to the class teacher if the face must stay off campus — the ID photo is still used inside school. CCTV: gates, corridors, buses; not toilets. Footage about 30 days unless an incident is under review.

5

Children under 18

The parent / guardian writes for a copy or a correction. We do not open a child-only email account for official notices. Special-needs notes stay with the special educator and the class teacher, not on a class WhatsApp.

6

Third parties

What the law or CBSE asks (LOC, results, transport permit). Payment gateways see only the fee. Uniform vendors do not get a marketing list. Cloud ERP, if connected, is under a school contract.

7

How long we keep it

Admission files: eight years after the student leaves, or longer if a law requires. Portal logins close when the TC is issued. Staff files: as PF / labour law. Demo web forms on this site are not stored on a school server.

8

Cookies and security

No advertising pixels. Portal, when live, uses a session cookie if you tick “Keep me signed in”. Passwords are never asked on a phone. A suspected leak: write the same day to [email protected].

9

Your rights

Copy, correction, or “no off-campus photo”. Email [email protected] from the registered address. Reply in 15 working days. Unanswered grievances: Principal, then the Data Protection Board when that channel applies.
