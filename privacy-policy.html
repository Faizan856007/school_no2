Privacy Policy

How Green Valley International School collects, uses and keeps information of students and parents.

This policy applies to the school website, the parent / student portal and forms submitted at the Admissions Desk. Last updated: August 2026.

1. What we collect

Name, date of birth, address, mobile, email, academic records, photographs for identity cards and the yearbook, and transport route if you opt in. We do not ask for Aadhaar to be published online.

2. Why we collect it

Admission, attendance, fee receipts, report cards, PTM booking, transport and statutory CBSE / government returns. We do not sell data to advertisers.

3. Who can see it

Class teachers and the office on a need-to-know basis. The parent app shows only your ward. Staff must not take student data home on a personal drive.

4. Photographs

Assembly and event photographs may appear on the school website and official social pages. Write to the class teacher if you do not want your child’s face used off campus.

5. How long we keep it

Admission files are kept for eight years after the student leaves, or longer if the law requires. Portal logins are closed when the TC is issued.

6. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the data we hold, or ask for a correction. Email [email protected] from the registered address. We reply within 15 working days.
