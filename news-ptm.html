Parent-Teacher Meeting — Saturday, 12 September 2026

Circular No. GVIS/ACAD/2026/22  |  Date: 12 September 2026  |  Issued by: Academic Office

To: Parents of Nursery to Class XII

The first formal PTM of the term will be held on Saturday, 12 September 2026. Unit-1 report cards will be handed over in the same meeting.

1. Booking a slot

  1. Open the parent app → PTM.
  2. Select two preferred 8-minute slots with the class teacher.
  3. Classes VI–XII may add subject-teacher slots if needed.
  4. A confirmation SMS will be sent by 8 September 2026.

Walk-in parents will be waitlisted. Please do not arrive outside the allotted window.

2. On the day

  • Enter from Gate 2. Parking attendants will guide you.
  • Bring the parent ID issued by the school.
  • Younger siblings should wait in the library lounge, not in the meeting room.
  • School transport will run on the Saturday timetable.

3. If you cannot attend

Inform the class teacher on the app by 10 September. A follow-up slot (online or after school) can be requested. Report cards will not be sent home with the child.

4. Issued by

Academic Office, Green Valley International School. Queries: [email protected] / +91 98765 43210 (9 AM – 2 PM).
