Academics · CBSE · 2026–27

Academic programs

Nursery to Class XII on one campus. English medium. Hindi from Nursery. Third language from Class VI.

ApproachWingsSubjectsAssessment

We follow CBSE. NEP-aligned competency work is inside the existing periods — we do not add a fourth “NEP period” that steals science. Academic Office: [email protected]

OUR APPROACH

How we teach

Clear lessons, enough time to practise, and an adult who knows the child by name.

Languages

English is the medium. Hindi is taught from Nursery. A third language is offered from Class VI.

Labs & making

Science labs, a robotics room and an art studio. Senior practicals are held on our campus.

Wellbeing

A counsellor is available on a written request. Class teachers write home every Friday in Primary.

OUR PROGRAMS

Three stages, one campus

Primary School

Nursery – Class V. Play, language and number. No formal exam till Class III.

Read More →

Middle School

Classes VI – VIII. Labs, coding, clubs and houses.

Read More →

Senior School

Classes IX – XII. CBSE boards. Science, Commerce, Humanities.

Read More →

Assessment

Primary I–II: observation notes. III–V: two reviews a year. VI–VIII: unit tests, mid-term, annual. IX–XII: as per CBSE, plus school pre-boards.

Books & stationery

The book list is issued in March. The school bookshop opens for one week in the last week of March. We do not force a single vendor.

Special needs

Share any diagnosis at admission. We offer extra time in internal exams where the board allows, and a quiet room for assessments.
