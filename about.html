About Our School

A CBSE day school in New Delhi. Strong classrooms, a green campus, and a quiet insistence that every child is known.

Green Valley International School opened in 2001. We take children from Nursery to Class XII on one campus and follow the CBSE curriculum from Class I.

Vision
