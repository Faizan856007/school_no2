Every child plays

Special needs wale bacche games se bahar nahi baithe. Diagnosis admission pe batao — PET + special educator saath plan karte hain.

  • Adapted PE in the same games slot: yoga, boccia, walking circuit, table tennis with a buddy.
  • Modified equipment (larger ball, lighter racquet, visual cues). No extra fee.
  • House points for taking part — Sports Day pe sensory-friendly seating, no sudden loud horn next to the child.
  • A child sits out only with a written medical note, not because “it will be easier”.
  • Quiet club option: Kalakriti (art + makers) — small group, same Thursday slot.

Special educator + PET · inclusion@greenvalley.edu.in · tell us at admission, not the week of Sports Day

RPWD / CBSE inclusive education ke under. We are a day school, not a special school — we still keep the child in the same house and the same Tuesday/Thursday club unless you ask otherwise.
