First Position — State Level Science Competition

Circular No. GVIS/SCI/2026/09  |  Date: 25 August 2026  |  Issued by: Department of Science

To: Students & parents

Four Senior School students of Green Valley secured First Position in the Innovation category at the State Science Fair held on 25 August 2026.

1. The project

The winning model combined a low-cost household water filter with a soil-moisture sensor for kitchen gardens. It was designed and built in the school Science and Robotics laboratories after regular hours over six weeks.

2. Recognition in school

  • Certificates will be given in the Monday morning assembly (1 September).
  • The working model will remain in the Innovation Hub till the Diwali break.
  • The team has been invited to the national round in November. Dates will be circulated separately.

3. For other students

Science Club meets every Wednesday, 2:30 PM to 4:00 PM, in Lab 2. Classes VI–XII may join with a note from the parent. No extra fee is charged.

4. Issued by

Head of Science, Green Valley International School. For media or certificate queries: [email protected]
