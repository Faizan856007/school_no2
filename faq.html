Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what parents most often ask about admissions, fees and school life.

A child must be at least 3 years old as of 31 March of the admission year to be eligible for Nursery. Age criteria for other classes follow the respective state board guidelines.

Admissions for the 2026–27 session are open now. Online registration typically closes in early March, subject to seat availability — apply early as seats are limited for most classes.

Birth certificate, Aadhaar card, previous school's transfer certificate and report card (for Class II onward), passport-size photographs, and address proof. A complete checklist is shared after the online form is submitted.

For Nursery to Class II, admission is based on a friendly interaction with the child and parents. From Class III onward, a basic assessment in English and Mathematics is conducted alongside the interaction.

Fees can be paid online through the parent portal via UPI, net banking or card, or offline by demand draft at the accounts office. See the Fee Structure page for full details.

Yes, GPS-enabled buses with trained drivers and attendants cover most areas of the city. Route and stop details are available on the Downloads page.

Families enrolling more than one child receive a 10% tuition fee discount on the second and subsequent children, applicable from the start of the same academic session.
