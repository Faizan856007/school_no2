Principal's Message

"Every Child Deserves A Classroom That Believes In Them"

At Green Valley International School, we believe education is not just about textbooks and examinations — it is about building character, curiosity and confidence. Our faculty is committed to nurturing every child's unique potential through a blend of academic rigour, innovation and personal attention.

With over two decades of experience shaping young minds, our teaching team works closely with parents to ensure every student grows into a thoughtful, capable and future-ready individual. I warmly welcome you to be a part of our school family.

— Dr. Anjali Mehrotra, Principal
